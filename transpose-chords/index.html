Change the key, not the whole sheet.

Singing too low? Capo too high? ChordPlane transposes a song by up to eleven semitones without scrambling the lyric layout. The chords, the diagrams and the key label all move together.

The transpose control stays out of the way

Open a song and choose Key. A sheet slides up with a wide slider and a live preview. Move it until the vocal sits comfortably, then dismiss the sheet. The lyric lines underneath never shift.

  • Semitone steps from −11 to +11
  • Sharp and flat spelling chosen from the original key
  • Capo shown next to the transposed key
  • Reset to original in one tap

Why it feels different

Most chord apps either rewrite the text or force you to recalculate every shape by hand. ChordPlane treats transposition as a layer.

Lyrics stay put

Chords are attached to syllables, so changing the key only changes the chord labels. The verse you already learned to read stays still.

Diagrams follow

Tap a transposed chord and the voicing sheet shows the new shape for guitar or ukulele. No mental transposition while you are playing.

Capo included

The capo value lives with the song. A capo 2 sheet and a +2 transpose produce the same sounding key with the shapes you already know.

Nothing is uploaded

Transposition happens locally, even in airplane mode. It is the same code path whether the song was typed today or imported last year.

Transpose questions

  • How far can ChordPlane transpose a song?

    Eleven semitones in either direction, enough to move most songs into a comfortable vocal range.

  • Does transposing change the original song?

    No. The original key is kept in the editor and the reset button returns the view to it instantly.

  • Can I transpose for a capo?

    Yes. The capo hint is stored with the sheet and shown alongside the key label while you play.
