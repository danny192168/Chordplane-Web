Offline guitar chords

Your chord sheets, readable when the signal is not.

ChordPlane is an offline guitar chord app for Android. It keeps the songs you paste on the device, lays chords above the words they belong to, and renders diagrams locally. No account wall, no loading spinner, no server between you and the next song.

Why offline matters at a gig

A setlist app that needs a connection is a liability in a basement venue, on a train, or anywhere the band plays louder than the router.

Open the song instantly

Every saved song is a local database row. Tapping a title opens the sheet immediately, whether the phone has full signal, no signal, or airplane mode enabled.

Read chords above the lyric

Chords sit over the syllable you play them on instead of being buried in a wall of text. Long lines wrap without losing the chord position.

Tap a chord for a voicing

Guitar and ukulele diagrams are drawn on the device. There is no image CDN to wait for when you need to check an F#m shape.

Keep the running order

Build a set, drag it into order, and move through the night with a tap. The set itself stays on the phone too.

Bring the sheets you already use

You do not need to retype anything into a special format. ChordPlane recognizes chordPro brackets, bare chord rows, section labels and the usual capo and tempo notes. Paste once, then play from it.

  • ChordPro: [G] inline with the lyric
  • Bare chord rows above the matching lyric line
  • Angle-wrapped groups such as <G D Em C>
  • Sections, repeats, capo and BPM

Offline by default, social when you choose

The library never uploads itself. Sharing is an explicit action that sends only the song or set you pick.

A cloud link is optional. When you create one, the recipient receives a snapshot of that setlist — not your whole library — and you can revoke the link later.

Questions about offline chords

  • Does ChordPlane need an internet connection to read chords?

    No. Songs, diagrams and transposition are handled on the device. The connection is only used when you search the shared catalog or create a cloud share link.

  • Can I import chord sheets I already have?

    Yes. Paste ChordPro, a plain chord row, or text copied from a message. Sections, capo and tempo are kept.

  • Will an account be required?

    No. Guest mode is the default. An account is only for cloud share links you can manage and delete later.

Put the songs in your pocket.

One signed APK, Android 7.0 or newer. Updates install over the old build and keep your songs.
