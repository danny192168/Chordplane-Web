What ChordPlane does with your data

Short version: your library never leaves the device. The only thing that goes to a server is a sheet you explicitly share.

Last updated 21 November 2026

On your device

Songs, set lists, transpose settings and preferences are stored in a local database inside the app. They are not uploaded, synced or backed up by ChordPlane. If you uninstall the app, Android removes that data with it.

If you create an account

An account is optional and only needed to create share links you can manage later. You can sign up with an email address and password, or with Google.

  • Email and password: the address is stored by our authentication provider (Supabase). Passwords are hashed, never stored in readable form.
  • Google sign-in: Google tells us your email address, display name and profile picture URL. Nothing else is requested, and we do not receive access to Gmail, Drive or any other Google service.

What a share link stores

Sharing is the one action that sends content to a server. When you share a song or a set list, ChordPlane stores:

  • the sheet or set list you chose to share, as a fixed snapshot;
  • your account's email address, so the share can be listed and removed;
  • when it was created, and a random token that forms the link.

A share link is public to anyone who has the URL. Treat it like posting the sheet: don't share material you don't have the right to distribute. Removing the share from the app deletes the stored snapshot, and the link stops working immediately.

Cookies and local storage

The app stores your sign-in session on the device so you stay logged in between launches. This website is static: it sets no advertising cookies and runs no analytics.

What is never collected

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No crash reporting or device identifiers.
  • No advertising SDKs, and no data sold or shared with advertisers.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

Removing your data

Songs and set lists: delete them in the app, or uninstall it. Shares: remove them from My shares in the app. To delete your account entirely, including any shares still attached to it, open an issue and it will be removed.

Children

ChordPlane is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created for them.

Changes

If this note changes in a way that affects what is stored, the date at the top changes with it.

Contact

Questions about any of the above: open an issue on GitHub.
