Paste the text, keep the structure

A typical ChordPro export is compact but hard to read on a phone. ChordPlane parses the markers and positions the chords over the words, while leaving the original source available for editing.

One app, several chord formats

Chord sheets in the wild are inconsistent. Each song can choose how its chords should be detected.

Auto

Reads ChordPro brackets, angle-wrapped groups and bare chord rows. Good default for mixed collections.

Ultimate Guitar

Keeps the conventions used by tab sites, including their section headers and chord placement.

Brackets only

Reads [Am] and <G D> while leaving ordinary lyric text untouched.

ChordPro only

Strict ChordPro parsing for sheets that should never be rewritten. Nothing else is treated as a chord.

ChordPro questions

  • Can ChordPlane open ChordPro files?

    Yes. Paste the text into the editor. Sections and chord positions are preserved in the readable sheet.

  • Does it also read plain chord rows?

    Yes. Detection is per song, so a strict ChordPro sheet and a rough-and-ready pasted chord row can live side by side.

  • Is the sheet uploaded?

    No. Import, storage and rendering happen on the device. Cloud features are opt-in only.
