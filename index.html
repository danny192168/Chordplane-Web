Your set list, ready before the first chord.

ChordPlane keeps chords above the words they belong to, moves a song into any key without turning it unreadable, and shows the voicing while you play. The library is a file on your phone; the cloud only appears when you choose to share.

Built for the way you actually play.

No account wall, no sync spinner, no menu between you and the song.

Sheets you already have

Paste ChordPro, a plain chord row, or a list copied from a message. ChordPlane keeps the sections, the capo and the tempo.

Transpose without rewriting

Shift a song from −11 to +11 semitones. Every chord, diagram and capo hint moves together, and the lyric line stays where it was.

Voicings on the device

Tap any chord for guitar or ukulele shapes. The diagrams are drawn locally, so they work offline and at any text size.

Set lists that keep order

Group songs, drag them into the running order, and open the next one with a tap. Nothing here needs a connection.

The sheets you already have still work.

Bring a lead sheet with bracketed chords, a plain-text chord row, a ChordPro export from another app, or a set list copied out of a message. ChordPlane detects the format, keeps the sections, and lays it out as something you can play from.

  • ChordPro ([G]) and plain chord rows
  • Section labels, repeats, capo and tempo
  • Nothing is uploaded in order to read it

Nothing leaves the phone unless you share it.

No analytics, no adverts, no background sync. Your library is a database file on your device, and it stays there.

Read the full privacy note →
  • An account is optional. It exists so a share link can be traced back to you and deleted later.
  • A share link stores exactly the sheet you sent, not the rest of your library.
  • Google sign-in receives only your email address, name and profile picture.
