Built for playing

Everything a sheet needs, nothing it doesn't.

The tools sit out of the way until you reach for them, and the sheet itself stays readable at arm's length under stage lights.

  • Chords above lyrics

    Write ChordPro, paste a sheet in from the web, or use the chord toolbar. Bracket and plain formats are both detected.

  • Transpose anything

    Shift from −11 to +11 semitones without the key turning unreadable. Zoom the text, then let the sheet scroll itself.

  • Chord diagrams

    Tap a chord for guitar or ukulele voicings, or open every chord in the song at once.

  • Set lists

    Group songs, drag them into running order, and copy the list when someone asks what you played.

  • Share with a link

    Send a song or a whole set list as a link or a QR code. It opens in the app, or in a browser for anyone who doesn't have it yet.

  • Offline first

    Songs live in a local database. No account, no sync, nothing leaves the phone unless you share it.

Privacy

Nothing leaves the phone unless you share it.

No analytics, no adverts, no background sync. Your library is a database file on your device, and it stays there.

Read the full privacy note →
  • An account is optional — it exists so a share link can be traced back to you and deleted later.
  • A share link stores exactly the sheet you sent, not the rest of your library.
  • Google sign-in receives only your email address, name and profile picture.
