Ukulele chords

Ukulele shapes, ready before the strum.

ChordPlane keeps ukulele chord sheets on the device and draws the voicings locally. Switch the instrument and the same song shows uke shapes instead of guitar ones — no downloads, no account, no wait.

Tap a chord, see the fingering

The chord sheet is the primary view. When a shape is unclear, tap the chord chip and a voicing sheet opens with finger positions for the selected instrument.

  • Guitar and ukulele voicings from the same chord label
  • Diagrams rendered locally, with no image server

Built for small sessions

Ukulele players often need one song, one shape and one more verse. The interface stays out of the way.

One tap to the shape

Chord chips in the sheet are tappable. The voicing sheet opens over the song and closes when you are ready to play.

Keys that fit a higher voice

Transpose up or down and the ukulele diagrams follow. The capo hint stays visible for players who mix the two.

Paste what you already have

Bracketed chords, plain chord rows and ChordPro sections all import. There is no separate ukulele-only file format.

Setlists for the song circle

Arrange the running order and share it with the group. Only the set you choose is sent, never your whole library.

Ukulele questions

  • Does ChordPlane show ukulele chord diagrams?

    Yes. Tap any chord to see guitar or ukulele voicings, drawn on the device.

  • Can I switch an existing song to ukulele?

    Yes. The instrument is a setting, so one stored sheet can serve both instruments.

  • Does transposing work with ukulele chords?

    Yes. Chord labels and ukulele diagrams move together to the new key.
